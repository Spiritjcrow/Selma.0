The simulated units have slightly different natural frequencies. Each responds only to its two neighbors. A numerical timestep advances the simulation; it is not a shared timing command to the units.

What this demonstrates

This is an educational coupled-oscillator model, not a reproduction of the Nature study and not a time crystal. It is the first software test for a later physical SelmaOS robot prototype.

Next hardware milestone: one 5 V actuator, one position sensor, one properly rated motor driver, and one microcontroller. Verify that the controller can read position and command safe small movements before connecting two or more units. Keep actuator power separate from microcontroller GPIO, and share ground only where the circuit specifications require it.
